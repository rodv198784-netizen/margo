Decorated cakes take hours. If your price does not include your time, the more cakes you sell, the less you earn per hour.

How we calculate: total cost = ingredients and packaging + hours × your hourly rate. Price = cost ÷ (1 − margin), so the margin is on the selling price, which is the real profit you keep.
